## Token Usage Guide

### What's a token?

Tokens are pieces of words used for processing text. On average:
- 1 token ≈ 4 characters
- 1 token ≈ 0.75 words
- 100 tokens ≈ 75 words

### Example Usage

**Chatbot message:**
```
User: "How do I deploy a React app?"
Assistant: "To deploy a React app, you can use services like Vercel, Netlify, or AWS..."

Tokens used: ~150 tokens
```

**Code review:**
```
Input: 500 lines of Python code
Output: Review with 10 suggestions

Tokens used: ~2,000 tokens
```

**Content generation:**
```
Input: "Write a blog post about AI"
Output: 1,000 word article

Tokens used: ~1,500 tokens
```

## Upgrading & Downgrading

**Upgrade:**
- Takes effect immediately
- Prorated charge for remainder of month
- Higher quota available instantly

**Downgrade:**
- Takes effect at next billing cycle
- Keep current quota until cycle ends
- No prorated refunds

**Cancel:**
- Takes effect at end of current billing period
- Keep access until period ends
- Can reactivate anytime

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I switch plans anytime?
Yes! Upgrade instantly or downgrade at the end of your billing cycle.

### What happens if I hit my quota?
Free tier: Service paused. Paid tiers: Overage charges apply or upgrade.

### Do you offer discounts?
- Annual plans: 20% discount (contact us)

Yes, you can purchase token packs:
- 100k tokens: $10

### Do unused tokens roll over?
No, tokens reset each billing cycle. Consider a lower tier if you're not using your quota.

### Is there a free trial?
The Free tier is always available for testing. No credit card required.
